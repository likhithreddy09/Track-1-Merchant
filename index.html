The problem

Merchants are blind on both sides of the shelf

They know yesterday’s sales. They don’t know neighbourhood demand — or which nearby shop can fill a gap or take excess stock.

Demand gap

They can’t see the market around them

  • How many homes sit within 1 / 2 / 5 km?
  • What age groups and lifestyles dominate nearby?
  • Which customer segments are underserved?
  • Where should they look for new customers?
Supply gap

Stock is stuck while another shop runs out

  • Excess inventory freezes working capital
  • Nearby merchants may need the same SKU today
  • No trusted way to discover or negotiate locally
  • Stockouts and wastage happen side by side
The solution

One platform. Two engines. One growth loop.

LocalIQ does not ship two unrelated tools. Demand tells merchants what the neighbourhood needs. Exchange helps them move stock. AI closes the loop.

Product pillars

Built as one merchant growth system

Hyperlocal demand intelligence and merchant-to-merchant inventory exchange — designed to feed each other.

Hyperlocal Demand Intelligence

Merchants enter shop location and discover the market around them — not just their own sales history.

  1. Map homes, offices and local activity within 1–5 km
  2. Understand age mix, household profile and demand potential
  3. Spot underserved segments and next-day / weekly outlook
  4. Get plain-language AI guidance on what to stock next

Merchant-to-Merchant Inventory Exchange

Turn one shop’s dead stock into another shop’s urgent refill — without waiting on distributors.

  1. Merchant A lists excess inventory with quantity and price
  2. Merchant B searches what they need nearby
  3. AI ranks matches by product, distance, urgency and price
  4. Both approve the deal and settle through Paytm
